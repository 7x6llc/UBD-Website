Expertise

Compassion and curiosity before judgment.

People are complex. No single model explains the whole person — which is why Diana has continued studying, long after any credential was strictly necessary.

Diana's work is rooted in the belief that everyone has a story — and that story should not become a source of shame. The experiences, decisions, adaptations, relationships, successes, mistakes, and circumstances that brought someone to this moment are part of the context in which she learned to survive, succeed, belong, protect herself, and make sense of the world.

The goal is not to reject that story. The goal is to own it, understand it, embrace what it taught you, and then decide what still belongs.

There is no shame in the life that brought you to this moment.
