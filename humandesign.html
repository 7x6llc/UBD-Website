About Your Human Design Report

What is Human Design?

Human Design is a system used for self-observation and experimentation. In Unbecoming By Design, it is used as a lens for exploring how you naturally move through energy, decisions, relationships, and areas where outside conditioning may become especially influential.

What information do I need to create a chart?

Your chart uses your birth date, birth time, and birth place. Birth time matters because the calculation is based on planetary positions at the time of birth.

What will the report show me?

The chart includes foundational Human Design information such as Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile, centers, channels, and gates. It is information to explore, not a prescription for who you should be.

Is my birth information stored?

No Account required. The chart and birth data stay on your device. Your name and email are submitted to Unbecoming By Design when you begin the report, but your birth date, birth time, birth place, and chart details are not sent to Brevo.
